How to use it

The path works on your own, but it was built to be taught from.

Steps 1, 3 and 4 project

All three are keyboard-driven and have a fullscreen toggle — arrow keys or space to advance, F for fullscreen on the later two. Type is sized to stay readable from the back of a hall.

Step 2 is not a lesson

Don't read PG Master front to back. Open it in a second tab at step 1 and search it whenever a function comes up — it is a dictionary, and the path is the grammar.

Nothing here needs installing

Every page is static and opens in a browser. You only need your own PostgreSQL once you reach step 5 and start solving for real.
